# Chapter 1 Management Information Systems

## 1. Data and Big Data

**Data**
**EN:** Data are raw facts that describe the characteristics of an event or object.
**CN:** 数据是描述事件或对象特征的原始事实。

**Big Data**
**EN:** Big data is a collection of large, complex datasets, which cannot be analyzed using traditional database methods and tools.
**CN:** 大数据是大型、复杂的数据集，不能用传统数据库方法和工具分析。

**Four features of Big Data / 4V**
**EN:** Variety means different forms of structured and unstructured data.
**CN:** Variety 是多样性，指结构化和非结构化数据的不同形式。

**EN:** Volume means the scale of data.
**CN:** Volume 是数据规模。

**EN:** Velocity means the analysis of streaming data as it travels around the Internet.
**CN:** Velocity 是对互联网上流动的流式数据进行分析。

**EN:** Veracity means the quality, accuracy, integrity and credibility of data.
**CN:** Veracity 是数据的质量、准确性、完整性和可信度。

---

## 2. Information, Business Intelligence, Knowledge

**Information**
**EN:** Information is data converted into a meaningful and useful context.
**CN:** 信息是被转换到有意义、有用情境中的数据。

**Business Intelligence**
**EN:** Business intelligence is information collected from multiple data sources such as supplier, customer, competitor, partner, and industry that analyzes patterns, trends, and relationships for strategic decision making.
**CN:** 商业智能是从供应商、客户、竞争者、合作伙伴和行业等多个数据源收集信息，并分析模式、趋势和关系，以支持战略决策。

**Knowledge**
**EN:** Knowledge is skills, experience, and expertise coupled with information and intelligence that creates a person’s intellectual resources.
**CN:** 知识是技能、经验和专业能力与信息和智能结合后形成的个人智力资源。

---

## 3. Data Silo and MIS

**Data Silo**
**EN:** Data silo occurs when a business unit is unable to freely communicate with other business units, making it difficult or impossible for organizations to work cross-functionally.
**CN:** 数据孤岛是指一个业务部门无法与其他部门自由沟通，导致组织难以或无法跨部门协作。

**Systems Thinking**
**EN:** Systems thinking is a way of monitoring the entire system by viewing multiple inputs being processed or transformed to produce outputs while continuously gathering feedback in each step.
**CN:** 系统思维是把整个系统看作输入经过处理或转换形成输出，并在每一步持续收集反馈。

**Management Information Systems, MIS**
**EN:** MIS is a business function, like accounting and human resources, which moves information about people, products, services, and processes across the company to facilitate decision-making and problem-solving.
**CN:** MIS 是像会计和人力资源一样的业务职能，它在公司内部传递关于人员、产品、服务和流程的信息，以促进决策和问题解决。

# Chapter 2 MIS Driven Business Strategy

## 1. Competitive Advantage and First-Mover Advantage

**Competitive Advantage**
**EN:** Competitive advantage is a product or service that an organization’s customers place a greater value on than similar offerings from a competitor.
**CN:** 竞争优势是顾客认为某组织的产品或服务比竞争者类似产品或服务更有价值。

**First-Mover Advantage**
**EN:** First-mover advantage occurs when an organization can significantly impact its market share by being first to market with a competitive advantage.
**CN:** 先发优势是指组织凭借竞争优势率先进入市场，从而显著影响市场份额。

---

## 2. SWOT Analysis

**EN:** A SWOT analysis evaluates an organization’s Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats to identify significant influences that work for or against business strategies.
**CN:** SWOT 分析评估组织的优势、劣势、机会和威胁，以识别支持或阻碍商业战略的重要影响因素。

**Memory sentence**
**EN:** Strengths and weaknesses are internal factors; opportunities and threats are external factors.
**CN:** 优势和劣势是内部因素；机会和威胁是外部因素。

**Examples**
**EN:** Strengths include core competencies, market leaders, cost advantages, and excellent management.
**CN:** 优势包括核心能力、市场领导地位、成本优势和优秀管理。

**EN:** Weaknesses include lack of strategic direction, obsolete technologies, lack of managerial talent, and outdated product line.
**CN:** 劣势包括缺乏战略方向、技术过时、缺乏管理人才和产品线过时。

**EN:** Opportunities include expanded product line, increase in demand, new markets, and new regulations.
**CN:** 机会包括扩展产品线、需求增加、新市场和新法规。

**EN:** Threats include new entrants, substitute products, shrinking markets, and costly regulatory requirements.
**CN:** 威胁包括新进入者、替代品、市场萎缩和高成本监管要求。

---

## 3. Porter’s Five Forces Model

**EN:** Porter’s Five Forces Framework helps identify the attractiveness of an industry in terms of five competitive forces: the threat of entry, the threat of substitutes, the bargaining power of buyers, the bargaining power of suppliers, and the extent of rivalry between competitors.
**CN:** 波特五力模型通过五种竞争力量判断行业吸引力：进入威胁、替代品威胁、买方议价能力、供应商议价能力和现有竞争者竞争程度。

### 1. Rivalry between existing competitors

**EN:** Competitive rivals are organisations with similar products and services aimed at the same customer group and are direct competitors in the same industry or market.
**CN:** 现有竞争者是指在同一行业或市场中，面向同一客户群并提供相似产品或服务的直接竞争组织。

**EN:** The degree of rivalry increases when competitors are of roughly equal size, competitors are aggressive in seeking leadership, the market is mature or declining, there are high fixed costs, exit barriers are high, and there is a low level of differentiation.
**CN:** 当竞争者规模相近、积极争夺领导地位、市场成熟或衰退、固定成本高、退出壁垒高、差异化低时，竞争强度上升。

### 2. Threat of entry

**EN:** Barriers to entry are the factors that need to be overcome by new entrants if they are to compete. The threat of entry is low when the barriers to entry are high and vice versa.
**CN:** 进入壁垒是新进入者必须克服的因素；进入壁垒高，进入威胁低，反之亦然。

**Main barriers**
**EN:** Economies of scale or high fixed costs; experience and learning; access to supply and distribution channels; differentiation and market penetration costs; legislation or government restrictions; expected retaliation from incumbents.
**CN:** 主要进入壁垒包括规模经济或高固定成本、经验和学习、供应和分销渠道、差异化和市场渗透成本、法律或政府限制、现有企业预期反击。

### 3. Threat of substitutes

**EN:** Substitutes are products or services that offer a similar benefit to an industry’s products or services, but have a different nature.
**CN:** 替代品是提供类似利益但性质不同的产品或服务，通常来自行业外部。

**EN:** Customers will switch to alternatives if the price/performance ratio of the substitute is superior or if the substitute benefits from an innovation that improves customer satisfaction.
**CN:** 如果替代品性价比更高，或替代品因创新提升顾客满意度，顾客更可能转向替代品。

### 4. Bargaining power of buyers

**EN:** Buyers are the organisation’s immediate customers, not necessarily the ultimate consumers. If buyers are powerful, then they can demand cheap prices or product/service improvements to reduce profits.
**CN:** 买方是组织的直接客户，不一定是最终消费者。强势买方可以要求低价或产品/服务改进，从而降低企业利润。

**EN:** Buyer power is likely to be high when buyers are concentrated, buyers have low switching costs, or buyers can supply their own inputs through backward vertical integration.
**CN:** 当买方集中、转换成本低，或买方可以通过后向垂直整合自己供应投入时，买方力量较高。

### 5. Bargaining power of suppliers

**EN:** Suppliers are those who supply what organisations need to produce the product or service. Powerful suppliers can reduce an organisation’s profits.
**CN:** 供应商提供组织生产产品或服务所需的资源。强势供应商会降低组织利润。

**EN:** Supplier power is likely to be high when suppliers are concentrated, suppliers provide a specialist or rare input, switching costs are high, or suppliers can integrate forwards.
**CN:** 当供应商集中、提供专业或稀缺投入、转换成本高，或供应商可以前向整合时，供应商力量较高。

## 4. Value Net and Complementors

**Value Net**
**EN:** A value net is a map of organisations in a business environment demonstrating opportunities for value-creating cooperation as well as competition.
**CN:** 价值网是商业环境中组织关系的地图，展示价值创造中的合作机会和竞争关系。

**Complementor**
**EN:** An organisation is a complementor if customers value your product more when they have the other organisation’s product than when they have your product alone.
**CN:** 如果顾客拥有另一个组织的产品时，会更重视你的产品，那么该组织就是互补者。

**Example**
**EN:** Sausages and mustard; Apple iPod and Sony Music.
**CN:** 香肠和芥末；Apple iPod 和 Sony Music。

## 5. PESTEL

**EN:** The PESTEL framework categorises environmental factors into six key types: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Ecological, and Legal.
**CN:** PESTEL 将外部环境因素分为六类：政治、经济、社会、技术、生态和法律。

**EN:** PESTEL helps to provide a list of potentially important issues influencing strategy. It is important to assess the impact of each factor.
**CN:** PESTEL 帮助列出可能影响战略的重要问题，并评估每个因素的影响。

**Political factors**
**EN:** Political factors include the role of the state as an owner, customer or supplier of businesses, government policies, taxation changes, foreign trade regulations, political risk in foreign markets, and changes in trade blocks.
**CN:** 政治因素包括国家作为企业所有者、客户或供应商的角色、政府政策、税收变化、外贸规定、国外市场政治风险和贸易集团变化。

**Economic factors**
**EN:** Economic factors include business cycles, interest rates, personal disposable income, exchange rates, unemployment rates, and differential growth rates around the world.
**CN:** 经济因素包括商业周期、利率、个人可支配收入、汇率、失业率和全球不同地区增长率差异。

**Social factors**
**EN:** Social factors include changing cultures and demographics, ageing population, income distribution, lifestyle changes, consumerism, and changes in culture and fashion.
**CN:** 社会因素包括文化和人口变化、老龄化、收入分配、生活方式变化、消费主义、文化和时尚变化。

**Technological factors**
**EN:** Technological factors include new discoveries and technology developments.
**CN:** 技术因素包括新发现和技术发展。

## 6. VRIO

**EN:** The four key criteria by which capabilities can be assessed in terms of providing a basis for achieving sustainable competitive advantage are Value, Rarity, Inimitability, and Organisational support.
**CN:** VRIO 用四个标准评估能力是否能形成可持续竞争优势：价值性、稀缺性、不可模仿性和组织支持。

**Value**
**EN:** Strategic capabilities are of value when they take advantage of opportunities and neutralise threats, provide value to customers, and are provided at a cost that still allows an organisation to make an acceptable return.
**CN:** 当战略能力能利用机会、抵消威胁、为顾客创造价值，并且成本允许组织获得可接受回报时，它具有价值。

**Rarity**
**EN:** Rare capabilities are those possessed uniquely by one organisation or only by a few others.
**CN:** 稀缺能力是只有一个组织或少数组织拥有的能力。

**Inimitability**
**EN:** Inimitable capabilities are those that competitors find difficult and costly to imitate, to obtain or to substitute.
**CN:** 不可模仿能力是竞争者难以或需要高成本模仿、获得或替代的能力。

**Organisational Support**
**EN:** The organisation must be suitably organised to support the valuable, rare and inimitable capabilities that it has. This includes appropriate processes and systems.
**CN:** 组织必须有合适的流程和系统来支持其有价值、稀缺、不可模仿的能力。

**VRIO Result Logic**
**EN:** Not valuable means competitive disadvantage.
**CN:** 没有价值 → 竞争劣势。

**EN:** Valuable but not rare means competitive equality or parity.
**CN:** 有价值但不稀缺 → 竞争平等。

**EN:** Valuable and rare but not difficult to imitate means temporary competitive advantage.
**CN:** 有价值且稀缺，但容易模仿 → 暂时性竞争优势。

**EN:** Valuable, rare and difficult to imitate, but not supported by the organization, means unused competitive advantage.
**CN:** 有价值、稀缺、难模仿，但组织不支持 → 未被利用的竞争优势。

**EN:** Valuable, rare, difficult to imitate, and supported by the organization means long-term competitive advantage.
**CN:** 有价值、稀缺、难模仿，并有组织支持 → 长期竞争优势。

# Chapter 3 MIS Driven Business Process

## 1. Six-Step Decision-Making Process

**1. Problem Identification**
**EN:** Define the problem as clearly and precisely as possible.
**CN:** 尽可能清楚、准确地定义问题。

**2. Data Collection**
**EN:** Gather problem-related data, including who, what, where, when, why, and how. Be sure to gather facts, not rumors or opinions about the problem.
**CN:** 收集与问题相关的数据，包括谁、什么、哪里、何时、为什么、如何；注意收集事实，而不是传言或观点。

**3. Solution Generation**
**EN:** Detail every solution possible, including ideas that seem farfetched.
**CN:** 列出所有可能解决方案，包括看似不现实的想法。

**4. Solution Test**
**EN:** Evaluate solutions in terms of feasibility, suitability, and acceptability.
**CN:** 从可行性、适合性和可接受性角度评估方案。

**5. Solution Selection**
**EN:** Select the solution that best solves the problem and meets the needs of the business.
**CN:** 选择最能解决问题并满足业务需求的方案。

**6. Solution Implementation**
**EN:** If the solution solves the problem, then the decision was correct. If not, then the decision was incorrect and the process begins again.
**CN:** 如果方案解决问题，说明决策正确；如果没有解决，说明决策错误，流程重新开始。

---

## 2. Three Levels of Decision Making

**Operational Level**
**EN:** Operational level employees develop, control, and maintain core business activities required to run the day-to-day operations.
**CN:** 运营层员工负责开发、控制和维护日常运营所需的核心业务活动。

**EN:** Structured decisions are situations where established processes offer potential solutions.
**CN:** 结构化决策是有既定流程可提供解决方案的情况。

**Managerial Level**
**EN:** Managerial level employees evaluate company operations to identify, adapt to, and leverage change.
**CN:** 管理层员工评估公司运营，以识别、适应并利用变化。

**EN:** Semistructured decisions occur in situations in which a few established processes help to evaluate potential solutions, but not enough to lead to a definite recommended decision.
**CN:** 半结构化决策是有部分既定流程可帮助评估方案，但不足以直接得出确定决策的情况。

**Strategic Level**
**EN:** Strategic level managers develop overall strategies, goals, and objectives.
**CN:** 战略层管理者制定整体战略、目标和任务。

**EN:** Unstructured decisions occur in situations in which no procedures or rules exist to guide decision makers toward the correct choice.
**CN:** 非结构化决策是没有程序或规则指导决策者做出正确选择的情况。

---

## 3. Using MIS to Improve Business Processes

**Automation**
**EN:** Automation makes business processes more efficient and effective and lowers operational costs.
**CN:** 自动化使业务流程更高效、更有效，并降低运营成本。

**Streamlining**
**EN:** Streamlining improves business process efficiencies by simplifying or eliminating unnecessary steps.
**CN:** 流程简化通过简化或删除不必要步骤来提高业务流程效率。

**Business Process Reengineering, BPR**
**EN:** Business process reengineering is the analysis and redesign of workflow within and between enterprises.
**CN:** 业务流程再造是对企业内部和企业之间工作流程的分析和重新设计。

**Magnitude of change**
**EN:** Automation usually creates operational-level change with lower business benefit.
**CN:** 自动化通常带来运营层面的变化，商业收益相对较低。

**EN:** Streamlining creates managerial-level change with medium business benefit.
**CN:** 流程简化通常带来管理层面的变化，商业收益中等。

**EN:** Reengineering creates strategic-level change with higher business benefit.
**CN:** 流程再造通常带来战略层面的变化，商业收益最高。

# Chapter 4 E-Business Values

## 1. Disruptive and Sustaining Technology

**Digital Darwinism**
**EN:** Digital Darwinism implies that organizations which cannot adapt to the new demands placed on them for surviving in the information age are doomed to extinction.
**CN:** 数字达尔文主义指无法适应信息时代新需求的组织注定会被淘汰。

**Disruptive Technology**
**EN:** Disruptive technology is a new way of doing things that initially does not meet the needs of existing customers.
**CN:** 颠覆性技术是一种新的做事方式，最初并不能满足现有客户的需求。

**Sustaining Technology**
**EN:** Sustaining technology produces an improved product customers are eager to buy.
**CN:** 延续性技术生产出客户愿意购买的改良产品。

**Examples**
**EN:** Disruptive technology examples include online streaming, digital cameras, and Internet-based business models.
**CN:** 颠覆性技术例子包括在线视频、数码相机和互联网商业模式。

**EN:** Sustaining technology examples include better phone cameras, faster processors, and improved laptops.
**CN:** 延续性技术例子包括更好的手机摄像头、更快处理器和性能更好的电脑。

---

## 2. Innovator’s Dilemma

**EN:** Innovator’s Dilemma discusses how established companies can take advantage of disruptive technologies without hindering existing relationships with customers, partners, and stakeholders.
**CN:** 创新者窘境讨论成熟企业如何利用颠覆性技术，同时不破坏与现有客户、合作伙伴和利益相关者的关系。

---

## 3. Web 1.0, Web 2.0, Web 3.0

**Web 1.0**
**EN:** Web 1.0 refers to the WWW during its first few years of operation between 1991 and 2003.
**CN:** Web 1.0 指 1991 到 2003 年间万维网早期阶段，重点是信息发布和电子商务。

**Web 2.0**
**EN:** Web 2.0 focuses on user participation, user-contributed content, collaboration, sharing, and social media.
**CN:** Web 2.0 强调用户参与、用户贡献内容、协作、分享和社交媒体。

**Web 3.0**
**EN:** Web 3.0 includes the semantic web, which describes things in a way that computers can understand.
**CN:** Web 3.0 包括语义网，它用计算机能够理解的方式描述事物。

## 4. Four E-Business Models

**Ebusiness Model**
**EN:** An ebusiness model is a plan that details how a company creates, delivers, and generates revenues on the internet.
**CN:** 电子商务模式是说明公司如何在互联网上创造、交付并产生收入的计划。

**B2B**
**EN:** Business-to-business applies to businesses buying from and selling to each other over the Internet.
**CN:** B2B 指企业之间通过互联网买卖。
**Example:** Alibaba supplier platform.

**B2C**
**EN:** Business-to-consumer applies to any business that sells its products or services to consumers over the Internet.
**CN:** B2C 指企业通过互联网向消费者销售产品或服务。
**Example:** Amazon.

**C2B**
**EN:** Consumer-to-business applies to any consumer that sells a product or service to a business over the Internet.
**CN:** C2B 指消费者通过互联网向企业出售产品或服务。
**Example:** Freelancers selling services to companies.

**C2C**
**EN:** Consumer-to-consumer applies to sites primarily offering goods and services to assist consumers interacting with each other over the Internet.
**CN:** C2C 指帮助消费者之间通过互联网交易或互动的网站。
**Example:** eBay, Facebook Marketplace.

# Chapter 5 Ethics and Security

## 1. Hacker Types

**Hacker**
**EN:** Hackers are experts in technology who use their knowledge to break into computers and computer networks, either for profit or just motivated by the challenge.
**CN:** 黑客是技术专家，他们利用知识侵入计算机和计算机网络，目的可能是盈利，也可能只是挑战。

**Types**
**EN:** Black-hat hacker, cracker, cyberterrorist, hactivist, script kiddies or script bunnies, white-hat hacker.
**CN:** 黑帽黑客、破解者、网络恐怖分子、黑客行动主义者、脚本小子或脚本兔、白帽黑客。

---

## 2. Virus and Malware Types

**Virus**
**EN:** Virus is software written with malicious intent to cause annoyance or damage.
**CN:** 病毒是带有恶意目的的软件，用于制造干扰或造成破坏。

**Types**
**EN:** Adware, malware, ransomware, scareware, spyware, worm.
**CN:** 广告软件、恶意软件、勒索软件、恐吓软件、间谍软件、蠕虫。

---

## 3. The Second Line of Defense — Technology

**EN:** There are three primary information technology security areas: authentication and authorization, prevention and resistance, detection and response.
**CN:** 信息技术安全有三个主要领域：身份认证与授权、预防与抵抗、检测与响应。

**Authentication and Authorization**
**EN:** Authentication and authorization determine who is allowed to access systems and what they are allowed to do.
**CN:** 身份认证与授权决定谁可以访问系统，以及他们可以执行什么操作。

**Prevention and Resistance**
**EN:** Prevention and resistance technologies help stop security attacks before they occur or resist them when they occur.
**CN:** 预防和抵抗技术用于在攻击发生前阻止攻击，或在攻击发生时进行抵抗。

**Detection and Response**
**EN:** If prevention and resistance strategies fail and there is a security breach, an organization can use detection and response technologies to mitigate the damage.
**CN:** 如果预防和抵抗失败并发生安全漏洞，组织可以使用检测和响应技术来减轻损害。

**Intrusion Detection Software**
**EN:** Intrusion detection software features full-time monitoring tools that search for patterns in network traffic to identify intruders.
**CN:** 入侵检测软件具有全天候监控工具，通过搜索网络流量模式来识别入侵者。

# Chapter 6 and Chapter 13 Project Management

## 1. Reasons for Project Failure

**EN:** Primary reasons for project failure include unclear or missing business requirements, skipping SDLC phases, failure to manage project scope, scope creep, feature creep, failure to manage project plan, and changing technology.
**CN:** 项目失败的主要原因包括业务需求不清或缺失、跳过 SDLC 阶段、未能管理项目范围、范围蔓延、功能蔓延、未能管理项目计划和技术变化。

**Scope Creep**
**EN:** Scope creep occurs when the scope increases.
**CN:** 范围蔓延是指项目范围不断扩大。

**Feature Creep**

# Chapter 12 System Development Life Cycle

## 1. SDLC Definition

**EN:** Systems development life cycle is the overall process for developing information systems from planning and analysis through implementation and maintenance.
**CN:** 系统开发生命周期是从规划、分析到实施和维护的信息系统开发全过程。

---

## 2. Seven SDLC Phases

**1. Planning Phase**
**EN:** Planning phase establishes a high-level plan of the intended project and determines project goals.
**CN:** 规划阶段建立项目高层计划并确定项目目标。

**2. Analysis Phase**
**EN:** Analysis phase involves analyzing end-user business requirements and refining project goals into defined functions and operations of the intended system.
**CN:** 分析阶段分析最终用户业务需求，并将项目目标细化为系统功能和操作。

**3. Design Phase**
**EN:** Design phase establishes descriptions of the desired features and operations of the system including screen layouts, business rules, process diagrams, pseudo code, and other documentation.
**CN:** 设计阶段描述系统所需功能和操作，包括界面布局、业务规则、流程图、伪代码和其他文档。

**4. Development Phase**
**EN:** Development phase involves taking all of the detailed design documents from the design phase and transforming them into the actual system.
**CN:** 开发阶段将设计阶段的详细设计文档转化为实际系统。

**5. Testing Phase**
**EN:** Testing phase involves bringing all the project pieces together into a special testing environment to eliminate errors and bugs, and verify that the system meets all of the business requirements defined in the analysis phase.
**CN:** 测试阶段将项目各部分放入专门测试环境，消除错误和漏洞，并验证系统是否满足分析阶段定义的业务需求。

**6. Implementation Phase**
**EN:** Implementation phase involves placing the system into production so users can begin to perform actual business operations with it.
**CN:** 实施阶段将系统投入生产环境，使用户开始用它执行实际业务操作。

**7. Maintenance Phase**
**EN:** Maintenance phase involves performing changes, corrections, additions, and upgrades to ensure the system continues to meet its business goals.
**CN:** 维护阶段对系统进行变更、修正、添加和升级，以确保系统持续满足业务目标。

---

## 3. Testing Steps

**Unit Testing**
**EN:** Unit testing tests individual units or pieces of code.
**CN:** 单元测试测试单个代码单元。

**System Testing**
**EN:** System testing verifies that the units work together as a complete system.
**CN:** 系统测试验证各单元作为完整系统能否共同工作。

**Integration Testing**
**EN:** Integration testing verifies that separate systems can work together and pass data correctly.
**CN:** 集成测试验证不同系统能否协同工作并正确传递数据。

**User Acceptance Testing**
**EN:** User acceptance testing determines whether the system satisfies user and business requirements.
**CN:** 用户验收测试判断系统是否满足用户和业务需求。

## 4. Software Development Methodologies

**EN:** Software development methodologies include Waterfall, Agile, Rapid Application Development, Extreme Programming, Rational Unified Process, and Scrum.
**CN:** 软件开发方法包括瀑布模型、敏捷开发、快速应用开发、极限编程、RUP 和 Scrum。

**Waterfall Methodology**
**EN:** Waterfall methodology is a sequence of phases in which the output of each phase becomes the input for the next.
**CN:** 瀑布模型是顺序阶段模型，每一阶段的输出成为下一阶段的输入。

**Agile Methodology**
**EN:** Agile methodology aims for customer satisfaction through early and continuous delivery of useful software components developed by an iterative process using the bare minimum requirements.
**CN:** 敏捷方法通过迭代过程和最低限度需求，早期并持续交付有用软件组件，以实现客户满意。

**Rapid Application Development, RAD**
**EN:** RAD emphasizes extensive user involvement in the rapid and evolutionary construction of working prototypes of a system to accelerate the systems development process.
**CN:** RAD 强调用户大量参与，通过快速、演化式构建系统原型来加速系统开发。

**Scrum**
**EN:** Scrum uses small teams to produce small pieces of deliverable software using sprints to achieve an appointed goal.
**CN:** Scrum 使用小团队，通过冲刺周期生产小块可交付软件以实现指定目标。

## 5. Classical vs Agile

**Classical Process**
**EN:** Classical process is anticipating. It has a disciplined, detailed plan, emphasis on long-term planning, focus on process, and is a plan-driven method.
**CN:** 传统过程是预测型的，具有严格详细的计划，强调长期规划，关注流程，是计划驱动的方法。

**Agile Process**
**EN:** Agile process is adaptive. It is a compromise between none and too much process, has short-term focus, less documentation, code is documentation, focus on humans, and is agile.
**CN:** 敏捷过程是适应型的，它在没有流程和过多流程之间折中，关注短期，文档较少，代码即文档，关注人，并强调敏捷。
